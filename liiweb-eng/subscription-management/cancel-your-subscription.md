# Cancel your subscription

1. Sign in to LawLibrary, then open My account to view your subscription details
2. Under subscription plan, click Cancel subscription

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/ssb 1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Confirm downgrade

3. Review the date your paid subscription will end&#x20;
4. Choose the main reason for the change and add any optional comments
5. Click **Confirm downgrade**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/subscription 2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Your **My account** page will show an Upcoming change card with the date your plan will move to My LawLibrary FREE.

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/subscription 4.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

N/B: **Cancelling the subscription does not delete your LawLibrary account. Your account continues on My LawLibrary FREE after the paid period ends.**

### Changed your mind?

You can cancel the pending downgrade before it takes effect

7. Go to the Upcoming change section
8. Click Cancel change.
9. Select OK to confirm

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/subscription 5.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10. A confirmation message appears when the pending change has been cancelled
