# Manage members and seats

The **Members** page brings your organisation's people and paid seats together. Owners and administrators can invite members, control administrative access, assign the right plan and remove people who no longer need access.

## Open the Members page

1. Log in to LawLibrary.
2. Click your name in the top-right menu.
3. Click **Manage [organisation name]**.
4. Click **Members**.

The page shows active members, pending invitations, assigned plans and scheduled changes. It also shows permitted usage information when the organisation uses **Managed usage** privacy.

## Invite a member

1. Click **Invite member**.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Choose **Member** or **Administrator** under **Role**.
4. Choose a paid plan, or choose no plan if the person does not need a paid seat.
5. Click **Review invitation**.
6. Review the possible billing change.
7. Click **Send invitation**.

Sending an invitation does not reserve or buy a seat. When the person accepts, LawLibrary first uses an unused seat on the same plan. If there is no matching unused seat, the organisation authorises the cost of a new one.

The invitation expires after 14 days. Under **Pending invitations**, use **Resend** to send it again or **Cancel** if the person should no longer join.

## Change a member's role

1. Find the person on the **Members** page.
2. Click **Manage**.
3. Choose **Make administrator** or **Make member**.
4. Review the change.
5. Click **Confirm change**.

Changing a role does not change the person's seat or plan. The affected person and organisation administrators are notified.

## Assign or change a seat

1. Find the person on the **Members** page.
2. Click **Manage**.
3. Under **Seat and plan**, click **Assign seat** or **Manage seat**.
4. Choose the plan or seat action.
5. Click **Review change**.
6. Check when the change will happen and how it affects billing.
7. Click **Confirm change**.

The preview can show:

- the amount due now;
- the amount added to the next card payment;
- any credit created; and
- the next recurring total.

LawLibrary recalculates these amounts when you confirm the change.

An upgrade normally starts immediately. A downgrade starts at the organisation's next renewal. Use **Cancel scheduled seat change** if the future change is no longer needed.

If you reassign an unused seat, it must be on the same plan to be reused without buying another seat. A difference in the new member's discount can still create a charge or credit. Review the financial preview before you confirm the assignment.

## Choose the right removal action

These actions have different results:

| Action | Membership | Organisation-funded access | Seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unassign seat now** | Continues | Ends now | Becomes unused |
| **End seat at renewal** | Continues | Continues until renewal | Ends at renewal |
| **Remove member now** | Ends now | Ends now | Becomes unused |
| **Remove member at renewal** | Continues until renewal | Continues until renewal | Becomes unused at renewal |

Removing a member or unassigning a seat does not automatically end the organisation's payment commitment for that seat. Use **End seat at renewal** if the organisation will not need it again.

## Unassign or end a seat

Use **Unassign seat now** when a member should lose organisation-funded access but the organisation wants to keep the paid seat for someone else. The seat becomes unused and can be assigned to another member on the same plan.

Use **End seat at renewal** when the organisation no longer wants to pay for that seat after the current billing period. Until then, the seat remains part of the organisation's paid commitment.

## Remove a member

1. Find the person on the **Members** page.
2. Click **Manage**.
3. Choose **Remove member now** or **Remove member at renewal**.
4. Review when their organisation-funded access will end.
5. Click **Confirm change**.

**Remove member now** takes effect as soon as you confirm it. **Remove member at renewal** leaves the membership and organisation-funded access in place until the date shown on the confirmation page.

Removing a person does not delete their LawLibrary account or saved research. If the organisation funds their plan, their account moves to the free plan when that funding ends. If they pay for their own subscription, it continues unchanged.

The released seat remains available unless you also end it at renewal.

An administrator cannot remove or demote the owner.

## Transfer ownership

The owner must transfer ownership before leaving the organisation or deleting their account.

1. Open the future owner's member details.
2. Click **Transfer ownership**.
3. Review the change carefully.
4. Click **Confirm change**.

The new owner receives all owner rights. The previous owner remains in the organisation in the role shown by the confirmation page.

## Understand the usage summary

For an organisation with **Managed usage** privacy, the **Members** page may show:

- whether the account has been activated;
- the last login date; and
- current counts for saved documents, folders, search alerts and followed topics.

It never shows document titles, searches, annotations, chats, downloads or research history. For **Billing only** privacy, no member usage fields are shown.

## Resolve common invitation problems

- If the invitation expired, send a new one.
- If the email address is wrong, cancel the invitation and invite the correct address.
- If the person already belongs to another organisation, they must leave it before accepting.
- If the organisation is waiting for its opening payment, the person can accept but paid access waits for settlement.
