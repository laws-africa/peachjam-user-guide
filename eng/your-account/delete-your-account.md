# Delete your account

If you no longer want to use %%APPNAME%%, you can delete your account from the **My Account** page. Deleting your account cannot be reversed.

(% if PAID_SUBSCRIPTIONS %)
If you only want to stop paying for your individual subscription, you may prefer to cancel it and use the free plan instead of deleting your account.
(% endif %)

## Important

- Account deletion cannot be reversed.
- Make sure you want to permanently delete your account before you continue.
(% if PAID_SUBSCRIPTIONS %)
- Cancel an active individual paid subscription before you delete your account.
(% endif %)
(% if ORGANISATION_SUBSCRIPTIONS %)
- If you are an organisation owner, transfer ownership before you delete your account.
- If you only want to leave your organisation, [leave without deleting your account](../subscription-management/join-or-leave-an-organisation.md). This keeps your saved research.
- Deleting an organisation member's account also ends their membership and releases any organisation-funded seat. It does not close the organisation.
(% endif %)

## Delete your account

1. Sign in to %%APPNAME%%.
2. Click on your name in the top-right menu and click **My Account**.
3. Click **Delete account**.
4. Confirm that you want to delete your account.
5. Enter a reason for deleting your account.
6. Check the box to confirm that you understand the process cannot be reversed.
7. Complete the deletion process.
